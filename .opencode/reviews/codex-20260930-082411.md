## Gameplay review

I inspected the live arena, road, and city at seed `a11ce5ee`, using **2400 × 2020 screenshots at DPR 2**. I drove and fired in practice and Division 5, exercised the highway’s pause and Controls flow, and entered city facilities. There are **three screen findings and one game-wide gap**, counted below.

### 1. Arena — combat happens without visible firing or impact effects

**Where:** The battlefield around the player’s car.

**What is wrong:** Holding fire consumes ammunition and damages opponents, but the world provides no visible shot path, muzzle flash, or impact effect. In Division 5, ammunition fell from **20/20 to 7/20**, front armour fell from **2/2 to 1/2**, and the feed announced **“Opponent destroyed — 2 left.”** The battlefield showed cars moving and overlapping without showing the exchanges causing those changes.

I checked the source after playing: the arena’s render list submits vehicle sprites and shadows, without projectiles or combat effects. This confirms the absence beyond a still that might miss a brief flash. [Screenshot during firing](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/.shots/review-20260930-1324/review-arena-firing-live.png)

**Why it matters:** Players cannot readily connect firing, incoming damage, and successful hits to positions in the world. Learning weapon direction and deciding how to evade requires watching HUD counters during combat.

**What I would do:** **Code change.** Render travelling projectiles from their simulation positions, emit a short muzzle effect on each shot, and show an impact effect where a hit resolves. Use the existing atlas muzzle-flash and impact-spark assets; new art is not required for the first fix. Effects should follow actual combat outcomes.

### 2. Road — even the 30-mile introductory route takes about 26 minutes

**Where:** The highway itself, reflected in the top-centre route progress and bottom-centre odometer.

**What is wrong:** At a steady displayed **70 mph**, route progress advanced **0.234 miles in 12.01 seconds**. That implies approximately **25.7 real minutes for 30 miles**, before acceleration, stops, or fights.

This is a pacing judgment supported by measured behavior. The empty starting bar is correct; the issue is how long completing it demands.

**Why it matters:** One journey consumes a substantial play session and delays returning to jobs, repairs, and building cars. Save and quit makes travel interruptible, but does not reduce that commitment.

**What I would do:** **Code and tuning change.** Compress uneventful travel so this route takes roughly **3–5 minutes**, returning to normal simulation speed around encounters, hazards, and player interventions. Advance route progress, calendar time, and resource consumption consistently during compression. Keep encounters at their defined route positions rather than stepping past them.

### 3. Arena — destroyed opponents remain hostile radar contacts

**Where:** The bottom-left RADAR panel, after a kill.

**What is wrong:** After **“Opponent destroyed — 2 left,”** the radar continued showing **three orange hostile markers** and reporting **three hostile vehicle contacts**. I resumed and moved again; the discrepancy persisted beyond the kill frame. [Post-kill screenshot](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/.shots/review-20260930-1324/review-arena-post-kill.png)

The source explains it: defeated vehicles remain in the opponents map, and radar contact generation does not exclude vehicles marked `destroyed`. The battlefield renderer does exclude them.

**Why it matters:** The radar directs attention toward a threat that no longer exists. Players may turn toward an invisible opponent or distrust the instrument precisely when it should help them find the remaining enemies.

**What I would do:** **Code change.** Exclude destroyed vehicles from hostile radar contacts. If wrecks should remain detectable, represent them explicitly as non-hostile wreck contacts and render the corresponding wreck in the world.

## Game-wide gap

### 4. The Federal Building has no core gameplay service yet

Entering the Federal Building opens **“Federal Building isn’t open for business yet — coming in a future phase,”** with Leave as the available action. Its approach label correctly says **closed**. This is an acknowledged content gap, rather than broken navigation. [Facility panel](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/.shots/review-20260930-1324/review-city-east.png)

It limits the city’s useful destinations. I would address it through **code and authored content**: give the facility one complete service or mission loop with a clear objective, consequence, and reward before expanding its menu. Additional building art would not address this gap.

The review did not complete an entire highway journey or test other viewport sizes. I found no additional city-specific defect in the interactions inspected.

REVIEW-VERDICT: 4
