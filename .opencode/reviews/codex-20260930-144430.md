## Gameplay review

I inspected the live arena, road, and city at seed `a11ce5ee`, viewed full-resolution screenshots at DPR 2, and exercised driving, firing, city facility entry, and the road trip menu. I found **one minor screen defect and one separate content gap**. No major arena or road defect was established in the interactions tested.

### 1. City — informational menu rows print their message twice

**Where:** The Arena building’s championship schedule row, and the Federal Building’s central notice panel.

**What is wrong:** The Arena menu displays “City Championship upcoming: day 50, 134, 218” twice in the same row. The Federal Building similarly repeats its entire “isn't open for business yet” sentence in adjacent columns. These are visibly separate copies, including an italic copy on the right. See the [Arena menu screenshot](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/city-arena-entry-review.png) and [Federal Building screenshot](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/city-federal-gap-review.png).

**Why it matters:** The duplicate text consumes space and forces unnecessary wrapping. In the championship menu, it makes information the player needs for planning harder to scan. This is a small usability defect.

**What I would do — code change:** In the shared menu renderer, suppress the secondary reason when it equals the row’s label, and let the label occupy the available width. Preserve separate explanations for disabled actions such as championship entry, where the reason adds useful information. No new art is needed.

### Separate whole-game gap: 2. The Federal Building’s ordinary service remains unfinished

**Where:** City — the blue briefcase destination near the starting gate.

**What is wrong:** I walked into it. Its ordinary visit offers only the future-phase notice and Leave. Source confirmation shows this visit uses the placeholder facility implementation. This finding concerns its ordinary service; it does not establish that quest interactions there are absent.

**Why it matters:** It adds a destination to the city without adding an ordinary activity. The approach label correctly says “Federal Building — closed,” so this is unfinished content, rather than an undisclosed broken entrance.

**What I would do — code/content change:** Give it an explicit interim role: retain relevant quest hand-ins, label it “Quest deliveries only,” and open an interaction panel when applicable work exists. Otherwise, keep it as a dormant building until its intended service is ready. Its existing art is sufficient.
