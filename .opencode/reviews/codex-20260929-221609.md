## Ranked findings

**The city’s vertical controls are reversed.** That is the first thing I would fix.

I opened and visually inspected all eight supplied screens, changed bodies and weapon rows, completed purchases, and drove in the city, arena, and highway.

The deployment changed during the review. The findings below describe the initially captured eight-screen build unless stated otherwise. I reproduced the control reversal again on the replacement build. Its constructor text also remains below your minimum size.

### 1. City movement contradicts the controls

**Where:** City, while walking or driving anywhere on the map.

**What is wrong:** W moves the player south on screen; S moves north. Starting at the southern gate, S carried me toward Salvage at the northern end. W then carried me back south. This also occurred on the replacement build.

**Why it matters:** The player’s most familiar movement controls work backward. At the initial spawn, W also pushes toward the city boundary, making movement appear ineffective. This obstructs the first attempt to find a job or workshop.

**What I would do:** **Code change.** Make the city direction vectors use the renderer’s positive-Y-is-north convention, then remove the compensating Y inversion in the input adapter. Verify walking, driving, diagonals, and touch controls against the same screen directions. The [input adapter](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/src/app.ts:4013) and [city direction table](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/src/sim/city.ts:172) currently disagree with the camera.

### 2. The direct constructor entry charges for a car it discards

**Where:** Constructor opened through `?screen=constructor`, then City and Fleet after confirmation. **Initial build; limited to this entry path.**

**What is wrong:** I named a Subcompact “Review car,” installed a Machine Gun, and confirmed the $1,940 build. Cash fell from $2,000 to $60. City still showed Duster, and Fleet contained only Duster. The purchased car was not available.

**Why it matters:** Anyone using that supplied deep link can lose almost their entire starting budget without receiving the vehicle.

**What I would do:** **Code change.** Use the ordinary construction completion handler for this entry path. The [direct-screen callback](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/src/app.ts:6156) discards the confirmed design and retains the original vehicle and fleet.

I separately completed the same purchase through New Driver. That path created the car correctly, so this finding does **not** establish a broken ordinary purchase flow. On the replacement deployment, the direct constructor URL opened Title instead.

### 3. Build approval and job eligibility give conflicting guidance

**Where:** Constructor’s right-hand LEGALITY panel, City’s bottom-left vehicle strip, and Courier Guild’s contract rows.

**What is wrong:** Through the normal New Driver flow, I built a car with zero armour and a Machine Gun containing `0 / 20` rounds. Constructor said “No violations — ready to build.” City then called it “Not road-legal.” All three available courier contracts refused it with “Your vehicle isn’t dangerous enough for this route.”

That refusal actually means **the car has no remaining armour**. The [eligibility check](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/src/sim/courier.ts:558) checks armour, rather than weapon strength.

**Why it matters:** The player can spend most of their budget on an approved build, reach the job board, and discover that they cannot take a contract. The wording points them toward more weapons when armour is the relevant requirement.

**What I would do:** **Code change.**

- Separate “valid design” from travel readiness.
- Before purchase, warn explicitly about zero armour and unloaded ammunition weapons.
- Make the city’s readiness wording agree with that distinction.
- Replace the courier refusal with “This route requires armour. Your car has 0 armour points.”

For a design built without armour, explain that the player needs an armoured build. Garage repairs only restore the original design’s armour; they cannot add armour that was never purchased.

### 4. Important text is substantially below the required readable size

**Where:** Constructor rows and instructions; Arena and Road CONDITION values, radar orientation, and message feed.

**What is wrong:** In the initial build, measured constructor row text was **12.1875 CSS px**. CONDITION values and radar orientation text were **10.3125px**. The constructor’s instruction line was visibly smaller still.

The replacement build raised constructor rows to **14px**, which remains below your 18px minimum.

**Why it matters:** These are purchase decisions, damage values, and operating instructions. During driving, the player must divert attention from the world to decipher them.

**What I would do:** **Code change.** Keep the root at least 16px, use 20px for ordinary controls and copy, and enforce an 18px floor for subordinate text. Widen or reorganize the HUD and let constructor rows scroll as needed. The original [token definitions](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/src/ui/tokens.css:199) also applied a reduced font size to the root, shrinking nested rem values again.

### 5. Constructor onboarding tells the player to press a key that does nothing

**Where:** Constructor, right-hand LEGALITY panel beneath the schematic. **Initial build.**

**What is wrong:** The instruction says “Mount at least one weapon — press Enter on a Weapon row.” I selected Weapon 1 and pressed Enter twice. It remained empty. Right installed the Machine Gun.

**Why it matters:** A new player following the instruction cannot complete the requested setup and has good reason to think the constructor is broken.

**What I would do:** **Code change.** Replace it with “Select a Weapon row, then press Right to choose a weapon.” Teach Facing and Ammo after installation. Enter only confirms when the Confirm row is selected, as the [handler](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/src/ui/builder.ts:471) specifies.

### 6. Escape silently becomes a driving binding during rebinding

**Where:** Controls, after activating the `driveUp` row.

**What is wrong:** On the replacement build, I opened the key-capture state and pressed Escape to back out. The row changed to `driveUp: Escape`; W and Up were replaced. I restored the classic preset afterward.

**Why it matters:** A conventional attempt to cancel changes the player’s movement controls. The resulting driving failure can look like a gameplay fault.

**What I would do:** **Code change.** Make Escape cancel capture and retain the previous binding. Provide a visible Cancel control. Also render player-facing names such as “Move north” and “Next weapon,” with keys shown as “W / Up” and “E,” rather than internal identifiers such as `driveUp`, `KeyW`, and `cycleWeaponNext`.

### 7. City markers cannot identify the service the player needs

**Where:** City building entrances and the bottom-right Fight / Build / Care / Jobs legend. **Initial build.**

**What is wrong:** Federal Building, Courier Guild, and Truckstop share the blue Jobs suitcase. Assembly, Garage, Weapon Shop, and Salvage share the yellow workshop marker. Individual facility names are not exposed before entry; the source confirms there is no hover or pointer-selection information.

I drove into the nearby Jobs-marked Federal Building and received its “future phase” notice.

**Why it matters:** Finding the actual job board or constructor requires visiting visually equivalent markers. Some visits lead to unavailable services.

**What I would do:** **Code change.** Keep the existing art and category colours. Show the facility’s specific name near its entrance when approached, and on pointer hover or selection. Identify unavailable services before the player enters them. This does not require recolouring the buildings or buying new art.

## Gaps in the game as a whole

- **A player-controlled highway pause.** Escape did nothing during the initial Road test, and the road input and frame paths have no player pause branch. Add a pause menu that stops simulation and provides Resume, Controls, and a clearly explained exit action.
- **Some advertised city services remain future content.** Federal Building explicitly reported this during play. The source also routes several other facility types through the future-content handler, although they can have quest interactions. I would finish the services needed by the main loop first and label the others before entry.
- **A stable, identifiable review deployment.** During this session, the title, constructor, typography, and city rendering changed substantially. The supplied Controls, Constructor, and City URLs subsequently opened Title. Restore the screen-entry contract and expose a build identifier so screenshots and findings can be tied to a specific release.
