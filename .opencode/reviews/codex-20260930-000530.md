## Gameplay review

The biggest screen problem is finding the right city service. The biggest game-wide problem is highway travel time.

I reviewed the live deployment with seed `a11ce5ee`, opened all three requested screens, and inspected **2400×2020 screenshots at the browser’s native 2× pixel ratio**. I drove in each screen, entered city buildings, and tested firing in the practice arena. I checked the deployed JavaScript where the visual evidence needed confirmation.

### 1. City — the “Jobs” markers send players to services that do not offer jobs

**Where:** The blue briefcase markers around the plaza; the Fight / Build / Care / Jobs legend at bottom-right.

**What is wrong:** The legend promises “Jobs,” but the same briefcase marks different services. I drove into one and reached the Federal Building’s “coming in a future phase” panel. Another opened bus tickets, asking around, battery charging, rooms, and body armour. The map does not distinguish these destinations by name before entry. Multiple workshop services likewise share the same orange tools icon.

**Why it matters:** Finding a job or building a car becomes trial and error. This obstructs the basic progression loop, especially for a new player who reasonably follows the briefcase.

**What I would do — code:** Label the nearest facility before its entry trigger activates: “Courier Guild,” “Truck Stop,” “Federal Building,” “Assembly Plant,” and so on. Reserve the briefcase for job providers; give travel/rest services a different existing symbol. Mark unfinished services as closed.

### 2. Road — the asphalt follows the car sideways, leaving its markings behind

**Where:** The dark road surface underneath the car; lane markings and guardrails above and below it.

**What is wrong:** Starting on the horizontal highway, I drove north. The lane markings and guardrails left the frame, but a horizontal asphalt strip remained centred underneath the car. The deployed renderer centres the surface on the vehicle’s full position, while it anchors the markings to the route axis.

**Why it matters:** The road gives contradictory information about where the player is. Leaving the carriageway still looks like driving on asphalt, and the player can lose the visible route entirely.

**What I would do — code:** Centre the asphalt on the vehicle’s **projection onto the route centreline**, preserving the route’s fixed lateral position. Use the same origin and heading for the surface, paint, and furniture. Once the car leaves that strip, show the actual verge underneath it.

[After driving north](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/artifacts/gameplay-review-2026-09-30/road-sideways-confirmation.png)

### 3. Road and arena — the WEAPONS panel covers the Arcade navigation link

**Where:** Top-left, behind the WEAPONS panel.

**What is wrong:** At the reviewed viewport, Arcade occupies approximately `(8, 8, 87, 44)` CSS pixels. WEAPONS starts at `(12, 12)` and covers the link’s icon and text. Pointer hit testing at `(40, 25)` reaches the WEAPONS heading.

**Why it matters:** A navigation control exists but is concealed and obstructed. This is particularly damaging on road, which lacks the arena’s visible “Exit to Title” button.

**What I would do — code/CSS:** Reserve the upper-left navigation area and place WEAPONS below it, starting around `y=64px`. Give navigation its own layout slot so changing weapon-panel height cannot cover it.

[Road screenshot](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/artifacts/gameplay-review-2026-09-30/road-initial.png)

### 4. Arena, road, and city — enlarged ground texels overwhelm the finer artwork

**Where:** Across the pavement and asphalt, particularly the large mottled patches around the car.

**What is wrong:** The ground contains conspicuous square texels and abrupt rectangular changes in grain. Buildings, thin cracks, and interface text have much finer detail. Driving makes those coarse blocks scroll prominently through the scene; they read as magnified texture pixels rather than surface detail.

**Why it matters:** The floor competes with the car and entrance markers for attention. It also weakens the otherwise coherent environmental palette.

**What I would do — rendering code first:** Put ground textures in a dedicated wrapping texture with linear filtering and mipmaps instead of enlarging nearest-filtered atlas samples. Smooth the transitions between grain patches. Preserve the arena’s slab joints and world-fixed decals so motion remains readable.

[Driven arena screenshot](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/artifacts/gameplay-review-2026-09-30/arena-driven.png)

### 5. Arena, road, and city — the unarmed car visibly carries a roof gun

**Where:** The circular mount and barrel on the player car’s roof.

**What is wrong:** Duster visibly has a gun, while WEAPONS correctly says “— none fitted” and the city strip says “0 mounted.” Holding Space in the arena produces no firing. The deployed car renderer selects its artwork by body type; the gun is baked into that artwork.

**Why it matters:** The vehicle’s most obvious combat feature contradicts its equipment. A new player can reasonably suspect that firing is broken.

**What I would do — new art plus code:** Make an unarmed base sprite for the subcompact, then draw weapon fittings according to the mounted equipment. The unarmed starter should have a visibly empty roof.

[Unarmed firing check](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/artifacts/gameplay-review-2026-09-30/arena-fire-unarmed.png)

### 6. Road — guardrail artwork is distorted into repeating comb-shaped panels

**Where:** Both verges, beside the white edge lines.

**What is wrong:** The rails appear as broad grey panels with ladder-like bars. The existing atlas artwork is a **96×21 horizontal rail**, but its quad assigns the long dimension to local Y and rotates it a quarter-turn. The geometry runs along the road while the texture is stretched across the wrong axes.

**Why it matters:** These are prominent, repeated objects intended to communicate highway speed. Their distortion makes the roadside look mechanically wrong throughout the drive.

**What I would do — code:** Match the quad to the texture: local X length `5.5m`, local Y thickness `5.5 × 21/96m`, then rotate by the route heading. The existing artwork is sufficient.

## Gaps in the game as a whole

**Manual highway travel has real-world pacing.** After 18 seconds driving east, the odometer showed approximately `0.3mi`; the destination still read `150mi remaining`. Duster reached `70mph`. At that speed, the route requires **at least 2 hours 9 minutes** of continuous driving, before combat or stops. The deployed simulation uses physical miles and elapsed seconds, so this is a pacing problem rather than an incorrectly empty progress bar.

I would add **accelerated travel between encounters**, advancing route position, battery use, calendar costs, and encounter checks together. Switch to ordinary driving as an encounter approaches. Target a few minutes for an introductory delivery. This requires gameplay code and balance work.

**Some represented facilities still lack an ordinary service loop.** The Federal Building I entered offered only a future-content notice and Leave. Source confirmation shows that these interiors can supply quest investigation actions when relevant, so they are not wholly nonfunctional. Their everyday service offering remains unfinished.

I would either give them a concrete service or show their closed/quest-only status on the city map. That requires gameplay and UI code.

I agree with the listed decisions about the radar, schematic preview, desaturated buildings, unbounded practice arena, and initial zero readings. None needs reversing to address these findings.
