## Gameplay review

I inspected the live arena, road, and city at **1200 × 1011 CSS pixels, DPR 2**, using full-resolution **2400 × 2022 screenshots**. I drove and fired in practice and Division 5, drove the road, and walked into city facilities. Below are **two screen findings and one broader game gap**, ranked within each category.

### 1. Arena — the camera excludes opponents during lethal combat

**Where:** The central playfield in a competitive arena match.

**What is wrong:** In Division 5, after three seconds of movement and held fire, the CONDITION panel showed driver health falling from **3/3 to 0/3**, with rear, left, and right armour depleted. All three opponents were outside the captured playfield. Radar correctly plotted them approximately **32–41 metres away**.

The camera shows only about **30 × 25.3 metres** at this viewport. I confirmed the fixed **40 CSS pixels per metre** camera setting after observing the fight. The machine gun’s configured range is **85 metres**, so combat can resolve well beyond the visible field.

[Match entry screenshot](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/.shots/live-review-20260930-1523/arena-division5-start.png) · [Damage and pause screenshot](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/.shots/live-review-20260930-1523/arena-division5-damage-and-pause.png)

**Why it matters:** The player can lose before seeing the opponents’ cars, weapon facings, or firing actions. Radar positions help navigation and approximate aiming, but those squares do not provide the visual information needed to read an exchange of fire.

This is a camera-design tradeoff I would change for competitive matches. The close framing makes the player’s car readable, but the observed combat cost is too high.

**What I would do — code change:** Make competitive arena framing respond to attacking opponents. Smoothly adjust zoom and camera centre to keep the player and the nearest attacking opponent inside the unobscured play area, with margin for movement. Keep the close framing for practice. When an attacker falls beyond the permitted zoom, show an oriented edge marker with a firing cue. This can use existing sprites and code-drawn indicators; it does not require new car art.

### 2. Arena — combat messages cover the pause instruction

**Where:** Top-centre, beneath the Division 5 controls banner.

**What is wrong:** The message panel and **“Esc — pause”** occupy overlapping space. The hint spans **y=88–110.8 CSS pixels**. After firing, the message panel spans **y=56–97.9**, cutting through the upper part of the hint. The taller, two-line match-entry message obscures almost the entire instruction. The screenshots show this overlap; the live element bounds confirm it.

**Why it matters:** The player’s first opportunity to learn the pause key coincides with the message that hides it. Firing produces another overlapping message during combat. Escape itself works—the problem is discovering it.

**What I would do — code change:** Put **“Esc — pause”** into the existing top controls banner and remove the separately positioned hint at `top:88px`. Reserve space below that banner for the message feed so its changing height cannot cover a control instruction. No new art is needed.

## Broader game gap

### 3. The Federal Building has no playable service

Walking into the Federal Building opened a panel whose only enabled action was **Leave**. Its notice explicitly says it is “coming in a future phase.” I also confirmed that its source uses the stub interior.

[Federal Building screenshot](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/.shots/live-review-20260930-1523/city-federal-building.png)

**Why it matters:** It is a destination in the city that supplies no activity or reward in the state reviewed. The approach label correctly announces that it is closed, so this is an unfinished content gap rather than a broken entrance.

**What I would do — authored content plus code:** Give it one complete federal courier contract using the existing courier machinery: acceptance conditions, a named destination, delivery confirmation, payment, and saved completion state. Establish that small playable loop before adding more branches. New building art would not address the gap.

I established no additional road defect during this pass. Road driving advanced the odometer, and the trip menu opened. I did not complete an entire route or establish balance across other seeds.
