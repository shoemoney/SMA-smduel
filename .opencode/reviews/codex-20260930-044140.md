## Gameplay review

I inspected the live **arena, road, and city** at **1200 × 1010 CSS pixels, DPR 2**, using **2400 × 2020 screenshots**. I drove and fired in the practice arena, drove the road and exercised its pause/Controls flow, and walked into city facilities.

These are the two screen defects I could substantiate, ranked by player cost.

### 1. Road — leaving the carriageway can leave the player without navigation

**Where:** The driving area, with the destination banner at top-center and radar at bottom-left.

**What is wrong:** After steering away from the initial horizontal carriageway, I reached a view where the road and its edge markers had disappeared completely. The banner continued to say **“En route to Albany · 150mi remaining.”** There was no arrow or distance telling me where the carriageway was.

The [off-road screenshot](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/.playwright-mcp/page-2026-09-30T09-43-08-649Z.png) shows the resulting state. I checked the screen’s controls and rendering code for recovery guidance; its radar reports contacts, while the route display reports progress. Neither locates the roadway.

**Why it matters:** A steering mistake removes the player’s main navigation reference. They must remember which direction they left the road, or search across repetitive terrain. Continuing toward the destination while displaced sideways can also leave them travelling outside the visible highway.

**What I would do — code change:** Calculate the nearest point on the carriageway from the existing route axis and the car’s position. When that point lies outside the viewport, display an edge arrow labelled **“Road — 35 m”**, pointing toward it. Show a smaller return indicator near the car while it is outside the shoulder. This needs no new art and preserves the ability to leave the road.

### 2. City — Amateur Night gives an eligibility instruction that an on-foot player already satisfies

**Where:** Inside the Arena building’s event menu, on **“Enter Amateur Night.”**

**What is wrong:** I walked into the Arena as the pedestrian, with the Duster parked elsewhere. Amateur Night was disabled with the reason **“Amateur Night is entered on foot.”**

Source confirmation explains the contradiction: eligibility rejects any non-null active vehicle. It checks whether the driver has an active car, rather than whether the pedestrian is currently walking. The actual requirement is therefore different from the instruction shown.

**Why it matters:** The player cannot work out the remedy from the message. The city explicitly teaches **G to enter/exit the car**, so “entered on foot” suggests that getting out should suffice. It does not.

**What I would do — code change:** If the intended rule is that Amateur Night serves drivers without an active car, keep that rule and change the reason to **“Amateur Night is for drivers with no active car.”** If walking into the building is intended to qualify, pass the pedestrian’s actual riding state into eligibility instead. The message and check must describe the same condition.

## Gaps in the game as a whole

### Highway travel still asks for a very large time commitment

The inspected road starts a **150-mile journey**. The simulation uses physical miles and seconds: at a constant **70 mph**, that represents approximately **129 real minutes**. This is a calculation from the live route length and simulation units; I did not complete the journey.

The trip menu now offers **Save and quit**, which makes that commitment interruptible. It still leaves a substantial pacing decision: how much ordinary driving should separate jobs, purchases, and encounters?

**What I would do — code and ruleset changes:** Target roughly **20–25 minutes for an ordinary leg**, shorten playable route distances, and adjust encounter density and battery drain together so encounters and resource expenditure per journey retain their intended weight. Treat this as a deliberate balance change, with measured before/after trip costs.

### The Federal Building is still unfinished content

I entered it and received **“Federal Building isn’t open for business yet — coming in a future phase.”** The [facility screenshot](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/.playwright-mcp/page-2026-09-30T09-43-56-931Z.png) confirms this directly.

Its approach label correctly says **closed**, so I would not report its availability as a screen defect. The remaining gap is the destination’s gameplay.

**What I would do — code/content work:** Give it one complete, useful transaction or mission interaction before presenting it as part of the playable service network. Until then, retain the closed approach label.
