## Gameplay review — ranked by player impact

I inspected the live arena, road, and city screens, captured and viewed **2400 × 2020 screenshots at DPR 2**, drove in all three, fired the arena weapon, entered city facilities, and entered Division 5 through the city. Source checks below confirm problems encountered in the running game.

### 1. Arena → City: autosave rejects the vehicle state silently

**Where:** On returning from the arena to the city. The failure is invisible in the city interface.

**What is wrong:** The live browser logged `autosave failed SaveMigrationError`, rejecting additional properties on the vehicle and its safe-city snapshot. The city continued normally without displaying a save failure.

The cause is concrete: driving adds `batteryDebt` and `controlLossSpinSign`, but the [vehicle save schema](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/src/persist/schema.ts:125) rejects additional properties and does not include those fields. This is a vehicle-state mismatch, independent of the screen-jump vehicle’s name or ID.

**Why it matters:** The rejected autosave cannot preserve that progress. Reopening the game risks losing changes since the last successful save, while the interface gives the player no reason to suspect anything failed.

**What I would do — code:** Add both fields to the save schema with validation matching their runtime values and compatibility defaults for older saves. Verify a driven vehicle through save, reload, and continued driving. Surface a failed save with a persistent, actionable message.

### 2. Road: one ordinary journey requires roughly two hours of continuous driving

**Where:** Top-center route banner and progress bar; bottom-center speedometer and odometer.

**What is wrong:** The Albany route starts at **150 miles**. About **80 seconds of acceleration and straight driving** covered only **1.6 miles**, reaching the car’s **70 mph** maximum. The banner still showed **148 miles remaining** after subsequent coasting.

At a constant 70 mph, 150 miles takes **129 real minutes**, before combat or stops. That estimate follows the observed driving rate and the route’s actual distance calculation; I did not complete the journey.

**Why it matters:** The city → job → highway → destination loop demands an exceptionally long session for one adjacent-city trip. For most players, travel prevents them from reaching the next meaningful part of the game.

**What I would do — code and balance:** Target approximately **2–5 minutes per ordinary leg**. Compress travel between encounters, then return to normal driving and combat near a contact. Advance route mileage, resource costs, and calendar time consistently through the compressed portions. Keep vehicle handling at its existing physical speed during encounters.

### 3. Arena: the advertised Space firing control does nothing

**Where:** Top-center instructions: **“Space/J fire.”**

**What is wrong:** Repeated native Space presses left ammunition unchanged. Native J presses fired the machine gun and reduced ammunition; further Space presses after cooldown still did nothing. The shipped bindings assign firing to **KeyJ**, with no Space binding.

**Why it matters:** A player following the prominent instruction can enter combat and fail to shoot. The alternative key exists, but the game should not make players troubleshoot its instructions under attack.

**What I would do — code:** Bind Space alongside J in both presets, prevent its browser scrolling behavior while gameplay owns input, and generate displayed key hints from the active bindings so rebinding cannot make the instructions stale. The road shares this input map.

[Advertised arena controls](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/.shots/advisory-arena-initial.png)

### 4. City: facility names arrive too late to help choose a destination

**Where:** The repeated orange Build, green Care, and blue Jobs markers; the bottom-center facility-name label.

**What is wrong:** The category markers are visible, but exact facility names appear only within the same radius that automatically opens their menus. Walking toward the nearby blue building revealed **Federal Building** as its menu opened. Driving toward the red entrance similarly opened the Arena menu.

The names are implemented; their timing is the problem. Several destinations share one category symbol, so players must enter facilities to discover which service each offers.

**Why it matters:** Finding repairs, construction, or work becomes trial and error, with modal interruptions during navigation. The distinction matters especially when one destination offers services and another opens an unfinished-content message.

**What I would do — code:** Show the nearest facility name at an inspection radius larger than the entry radius—start with **8 metres versus the current 3-metre entry radius**. Anchor the label beside that entrance. Require an explicit interaction to enter once nearby, and display its bound key.

### 5. Arena and Road: the weapon panel truncates even the starter weapon

**Where:** Top-left **WEAPONS** panel.

**What is wrong:** At the tested 1200-pixel-wide viewport, **Machine Gun** displays as **“Machin…”**. The active-slot triangle also wraps beneath the slot number, making a single weapon row occupy two lines. This persists while driving; it is not a loading frame.

**Why it matters:** The panel clips the information needed to identify the selected weapon while displaying its ammunition, cooldown, and condition in full. That impairs recognition when players have multiple weapons.

**What I would do — code/CSS:** Use two intentional lines: slot, facing, and full weapon name above; ammunition, cooldown, and condition below. Keep the slot number and selection triangle together with `white-space: nowrap`. Verify the layout against the longest shipped weapon names.

[Weapon panel during road play](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/.shots/advisory-road-east20s.png)

## Real gap in the game as a whole

**The Federal Building is an unfinished destination presented as an ordinary usable service.** I entered it through the city and received: **“Federal Building isn’t open for business yet — coming in a future phase.”** Its bright blue Jobs marker gives it the same availability signal as other destinations.

That is a content gap: this facility has no player activity behind its entrance. It also wastes a player’s navigation effort before revealing its status.

**What I would do — code/content:** Until its gameplay exists, mark it **“Closed”** during approach and stop automatically opening a modal there. Give operational facilities priority in navigation guidance. Implement the Federal Building only when it has a defined service or objective that contributes to the driving loop; new art is not needed for the interim fix.
