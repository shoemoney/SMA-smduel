## Gameplay review

I opened all three live URLs with seed `a11ce5ee`, captured and inspected **2400 × 2020 screenshots at DPR 2**, and exercised movement in each screen, including walking, entering the car, visiting buildings, and departing city. Source checks supported the observed behavior; I also checked the deployed bundle for the city input mapping, radar marker, and road surface placement.

### 1. City — vertical controls are reversed

**Where:** The player and car in the world.

**What is wrong:** **W/Up moves downward; S/Down moves upward.** Arena and road use the opposite, expected mapping. I reproduced this on foot and while driving. In these consecutive captures, pressing W moves the player toward the parked car below: [before](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/.playwright-mcp/review-city-s-up.png), [after](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/.playwright-mcp/review-city-w-down.png).

**Why it matters:** This breaks the most basic control expectation on the starting gameplay screen. Players trying to approach a building move away from it, and must relearn the controls when they reach road or arena.

**What I would do — code change:** Make city movement use the same Y-up world convention as driving and rendering. Update the city compass vectors and the vector-to-direction conversion together, removing the extra sign inversion. Verify W/Up and S/Down both on foot and in the car.

### 2. Road — asphalt follows the car after it leaves the actual route

**Where:** The highway surface around the centered car.

**What is wrong:** Driving north away from the initial east–west highway makes the lane markings and roadside furniture leave the view, but **an asphalt strip remains centered beneath the car**. The route’s paint stays in the world while its surface follows the player. Compare [driving along the route](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/.playwright-mcp/review-road-east10.png) with [driving away from it](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/.playwright-mcp/review-road-straight11.png).

This also creates misleading progress feedback: the odometer increases while forward route progress barely changes, yet the car still appears to be on a highway.

**Why it matters:** The environment tells the player they are following the road when they are travelling away from the destination. Once the markings disappear, recovering the route becomes guesswork.

**What I would do — code change:** Anchor the surface, lane paint, and roadside furniture to **one route origin and axis**, using the trip’s starting position. Extend the visible surface along that axis as the camera moves; preserve its lateral position. When the player leaves it, show the verge and a direction indicator back toward the route.

### 3. City → road — “Not road-legal” does not prevent departure

**Where:** Bottom-left vehicle strip, then the gate’s destination menu.

**What is wrong:** The starter Duster says **“0 armour · 0 mounted · Not road-legal.”** Nevertheless, I entered the gate, selected Albany, and drove onto the highway with no armour or weapons. The warning promises a restriction that the gate does not enforce. [Highway after departure](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/.playwright-mcp/review-road-from-illegal-car.png).

**Why it matters:** Players cannot trust the readiness indicator. It also lets them enter a dangerous route without the equipment the city tells them is required.

**What I would do — code change:** Use a shared road-legality check for the strip and gate activation. Disable departure with specific reasons such as “Fit a weapon” and “Add armour.” If unrestricted departure is the intended rule, change the strip to describe the actual condition—“Unarmed · unarmoured”—and stop calling it illegal.

### 4. City — entrance signs conceal which service the player is approaching

**Where:** The repeated blue briefcase and orange tool signs around the plaza; bottom-right legend.

**What is wrong:** The legend distinguishes broad categories, but the signs do not identify individual facilities. The blue entrance nearest the starting car led me to **Federal Building**, which only displayed a future-phase placeholder. Another blue entrance opened a working menu with buses, battery charging, lodging, and body armour. Both advertise “Jobs.”

The Federal Building’s unavailable status becomes visible only after entering it: [placeholder panel](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/.playwright-mcp/review-city-jobs.png).

**Why it matters:** Finding the desired service requires visiting and memorizing entrances. The nearest apparent job destination sends a new player into a dead end.

**What I would do — code change:** Keep the category colours, but add facility names beside the signs. Show a nearby service summary—“Truck Stop: travel, recharge, lodging,” for example—and mark unavailable facilities before entry. This can use text overlays; new building art is unnecessary.

### 5. Arena and road — the north-up radar triangle does not show vehicle heading

**Where:** Center of the bottom-left RADAR dial.

**What is wrong:** After turning and driving east, the car points east while the radar triangle still points straight up. The marker’s transform only centers it; it does not rotate with heading. [Arena after turning east](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/.playwright-mcp/review-arena-east.png).

**Why it matters:** A triangular player marker suggests direction. In north-up mode, its fixed orientation gives a false cue when relating radar contacts to the car’s front and weapon facings.

**What I would do — code change:** Rotate the triangle to the vehicle’s heading in north-up mode, accounting for CSS’s downward-positive Y axis. Keep it pointing up in heading-up mode.

The rings, sweep, crosshair, and marker are present. This finding concerns the marker’s behavior.

### 6. Road — roadside guardrails are stretched across the travel direction

**Where:** Repeated pale objects above and below the highway.

**What is wrong:** On the east–west road, the guardrail sprites extend north–south. Each occupies roughly **190 CSS pixels vertically and 40 horizontally**, producing repeated comb-like objects perpendicular to the carriageway. [Road capture](/Users/shoemoney/Projects/smduel/.playwright-mcp/review-road-east10.png).

**Why it matters:** These objects provide motion reference, but their orientation and distortion make the highway harder to read as a coherent place.

**What I would do — code change:** Correct the guardrail sprite’s local axis and dimensions. Render the 96:21 artwork with its long dimension along the route—for example, approximately **5.5 m × 1.2 m**, rotated to the route heading. Existing art should suffice.

## Gaps in the game as a whole

### 1. Travel pacing requires hours of driving

Ten seconds of forward driving from rest covered approximately **0.127 route miles**, reaching 70 mph. The simulation uses literal miles and seconds. At a sustained 70 mph, the 150-mile Albany trip takes approximately **2 hours 9 minutes**, before interruptions.

This is the most damaging overall issue I found. The empty starting progress bar is correct; the problem is how long reaching the destination takes.

**What I would do — game design and code change:** Separate campaign distance from physical driving distance. Compress uneventful travel between encounter sections, targeting a complete journey of several minutes. Preserve real driving and combat timing within encounters, and display campaign miles consistently.

### 2. Driving lacks a player-controlled pause, and road lacks an in-game exit

I pressed Escape while moving in arena and road; the simulation continued. Practice arena has “Exit to Title,” but road exposes no equivalent pause or return control. The input and screen code corroborate this.

**Why it matters:** Players cannot safely interrupt a driving session or back out of a mistaken departure through the game interface.

**What I would do — code change:** Add a visible pause button and Escape menu with Resume, controls, and Save & Exit. Define a road return or abandonment action with its consequence stated before activation.

### 3. Some visible facilities are still placeholders

I directly encountered the Federal Building placeholder. Source also routes Hotel, Story Office, Broadcast Studio, and Pet Shop to placeholder panels; those additional facilities were **source-confirmed, not individually visited in this pass**.

**Why it matters:** The city presents destinations with no playable service, weakening exploration and making its available scope difficult to understand.

**What I would do — product and code change:** First disclose unavailable facilities on the map. Then implement a complete service or quest role for each before presenting it as an active destination.

I would retain the deliberate decisions listed in the brief. These findings do not require adding arena walls, recolouring city buildings, collapsing indexed weapon rows, or treating correct initial instrument readings as defects.
